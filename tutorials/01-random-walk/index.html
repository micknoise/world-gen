What this shows

This starts in the middle of a wall-filled grid and walks one step at a time. Every visited cell becomes floor, which creates a simple cave-like layout.

  • Good for: simple cave-like dungeons and first procedural generation experiments
  • Watch for: narrow passages, uneven coverage, and large untouched wall areas
