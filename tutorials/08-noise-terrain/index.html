What this shows

This samples layered value noise across a grid and classifies the result into water, coast, plains, hills, and rock. A radial falloff makes the output read more like an island.

  • Good for: terrain masks, islands, and simple overworlds
  • Watch for: threshold tuning and repetitive patterns at poor scales
