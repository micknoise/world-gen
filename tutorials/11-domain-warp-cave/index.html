What this shows

Before sampling the noise field for floor/wall, the sample coordinates are themselves displaced by another noise field. This "domain warping" folds and twists the cave geometry, producing organic forms that plain noise can never make.

  • Good for: alien caverns, twisted underground spaces, lava tube networks, ancient ruins
  • Watch for: warp strength above 4 produces extremely fragmented results; sweet spot is 1–3
