What this shows

Plane waves at random angles and frequencies are superimposed. Where many waves constructively interfere, they form bright crests — those crests become the floor. No noise function is used at all: every room and corridor emerges from pure trigonometry.

  • Good for: sci-fi facilities, arcane or crystalline spaces, circuit-board-like layouts
  • Watch for: fewer waves produce bold regular patterns; more waves produce complex irregular rooms
