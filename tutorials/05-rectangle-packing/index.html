What this shows

This repeatedly tries to place random room rectangles without overlap. Once the rooms are placed, each room connects to its nearest earlier room to make a simple layout graph.

  • Good for: teaching room placement and density tradeoffs
  • Watch for: failed placements as the world fills up, and missing corridor carving

Rectangle Packing Rooms

Non-overlapping rectangles give you a set of room footprints.
