What this shows

Ridged fractal noise converts the crest lines of stacked noise waves into a branching network of passages. Each octave adds finer branching detail. Threshold controls how wide the passages grow.

  • Good for: cave networks, mine tunnels, dungeon corridors, underground rivers
  • Watch for: raise threshold to narrow passages; lower it to widen them into chambers
