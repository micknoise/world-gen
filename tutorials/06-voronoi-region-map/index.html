What this shows

This places seed points and assigns every tile to the nearest seed. The result is a hard-edged territory map that works well for regions, districts, biomes, or factions.

  • Good for: biome zones, territory maps, districts, and influence regions
  • Watch for: hard edges that may need later smoothing or terrain detail
