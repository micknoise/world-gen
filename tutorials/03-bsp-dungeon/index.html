What this shows

This splits the map into large rectangles, then smaller rectangles, then places one room inside each leaf rectangle. Corridors connect sibling branches, which gives a clear room-and-corridor dungeon.

  • Good for: readable dungeon layouts with clear rooms and corridors
  • Watch for: a regular, mechanical feel unless you add variation later
