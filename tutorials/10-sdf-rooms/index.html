What this shows

Rooms are circles, corridors are capsules (fattened line segments). Every pixel is tested against all shapes simultaneously using signed distance functions, then merged with a smooth-minimum that rounds the joints where shapes meet.

  • Good for: architectural dungeons, lab complexes, structured floorplans with organic joins
  • Watch for: increase smoothing for rounded blob-like merges; reduce it for sharp, distinct rooms
