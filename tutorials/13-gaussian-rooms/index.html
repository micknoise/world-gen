What this shows

Each room is a Gaussian probability density — a smooth bell curve centred on a random point. The density values from all rooms are summed at every pixel. Where neighbouring rooms are close, their Gaussians overlap and naturally merge, forming corridors without any explicit connection logic.

  • Good for: organic settlements, alien hives, fungal colonies, interconnected cavern clusters
  • Watch for: increase spread to merge rooms together; decrease it for isolated, distinct rooms
